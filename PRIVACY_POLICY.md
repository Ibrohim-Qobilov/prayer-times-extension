# Privacy Policy for Prayer Times Chrome Extension

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Prayer Times** browser extension ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information.

---

### 1. No Collection of Personal Information
**Prayer Times does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).**
* We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or geographical GPS coordinates.
* We do not track your browsing history, keystrokes, or search queries.
* We do not use cookies, web beacons, or third-party analytics trackers.

---

### 2. Local Storage (`chrome.storage.local`)
All user configuration settings are saved strictly on your local device within the browser's isolated local storage:
* Selected region / district for prayer calculations.
* Selected UI theme (Dark, Light, or Auto).
* Selected notification chime audio and volume level.
* Active language preference.

This data never leaves your computer and is immediately erased if you uninstall the extension.

---

### 3. Permissions Used & Justification
* `alarms`: Used solely to schedule accurate daily alarms at prayer times to trigger desktop notifications and chime alerts.
* `notifications`: Used solely to display desktop notifications alerting you when a prayer time arrives.
* `storage`: Used solely to save your local UI preferences (district, theme, volume) on your device.
* `offscreen`: Used solely to play audio chimes for prayer time alerts in the background.

---

### 4. Network Communications
The extension connects only to the official, public prayer times endpoint:
* `https://namoz-vaqti.uz` (The official calendar of the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan).

No personal information, device identifiers, or user telemetry is transmitted during these requests.

---

### 5. Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer user information to third parties under any circumstances.
